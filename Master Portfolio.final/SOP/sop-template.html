Client Attributes

Basic Use Cases for Collecting the Right Information

I kept these examples simple on purpose. The core job is to collect useful client attributes, store them somewhere the team can actually find them, and make them usable across service. SevenRooms supports guest profiles with allergies, preferences, tags, and reservation notes, while Toast stores guest contact information and order history and can import guest tags and internal notes. [web:31][web:35][web:38]

Use Case 01

Collect preferences

Keep basic details like seating preference, favorite table area, allergies, birthday, or VIP status in one profile instead of scattered across memory or paper notes. [web:31][web:37]

Use Case 02

Update notes after visits

Add short notes after an interaction so the next person understands the client better without needing a full handoff conversation. SevenRooms supports profile notes, reservation notes, and tags for this. [web:31]

Use Case 03

Use data during service

The point is not just to collect data. It is to make it visible when the team needs it, like at the host stand, on POS, or during follow-up. SevenRooms surfaces notes and tags for the next shift, and Toast stores searchable guest data including order history. [web:31][web:35]

What this solves

These are simple process improvements: less guesswork, cleaner handoffs, better recognition, and fewer missed details when someone new is helping the client. [web:31][web:35]
