Client Attributes

Basic Use Cases for Collecting the Right Information

The core job is to collect useful client attributes, store them where the team can actually find them, and make them usable across service. SevenRooms supports guest profiles with allergies, preferences, tags, and reservation notes, while Toast stores guest contact information and order history and can import guest tags and internal notes.

Use Case 01

Collect preferences

Keep basic details like seating preference, favorite table area, allergies, birthday, or VIP status in one profile.

Use Case 02

Update notes after visits

Add short notes after an interaction so the next person understands the client without a full handoff conversation.

Use Case 03

Use data during service

Make it visible when the team needs it — at the host stand, on POS, or during follow-up.

What this solves: Less guesswork, cleaner handoffs, better recognition, and fewer missed details when someone new is helping the client.
Process View

A Simple Attribute Collection Process

Collect details, store them in the client record, make them visible during service, and update them when needed.

    >Collect — Ask for a few useful details and add them to the profile. >Store — Save them as notes, tags, profile fields, or order-linked history. >Reference — Make them visible during the next reservation, order, or interaction. >Refine — Update the record over time so the profile stays useful.
